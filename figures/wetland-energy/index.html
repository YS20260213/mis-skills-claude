Energy is never lost. It only moves (transfer) or changes form (transformation).

Energy starts with the Sun

Plants are the doorway: they catch light energy.

Light energy Sunlight travels to the wetland and shines on the leaves.
Photosynthesis Leaves use light, water and air to make sugar (food).
Chemical energy Light energy changes into chemical energy. It is stored in the plant's leaves, stems and roots.
☀️ Sunlight70sugar units
🌿 No plants = no energy for animals. Plants are the start of the energy path.

Energy changes form in animals

Chemical energy from food becomes movement, stored energy, signals and heat.

chemical → kinetic

The dragonfly beats its wings about 30 times every second. Food energy becomes movement.

potential → kinetic electrical

A nerve signal (electrical) tells the legs to push. Stored energy becomes a jump.

chemical → kinetic + thermal

The hawk flies and keeps its body warm. Much of its energy leaves as heat.

🔥 In every change, some energy becomes heat and goes into the air.

A terrarium: a mini wetland

A small, closed garden in a glass box. It copies the wetland.

🔍 We can watch it. We see how energy and water move, without hurting the real wetland.
💡 Energy goes in and out. Light goes in through the glass. Heat comes out.
♻️ Things are reused. Water goes up, turns to drops and falls again. Old leaves become soil.
